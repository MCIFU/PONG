## Full description

```
The classic arcade Pong, now on your phone and your PC.

CHOOSE YOUR GAME MODE
• 1 player vs the AI, with 3 difficulty levels (Easy, Normal and Hard) that change the speed and intelligence of the machine.
• 2 players: share the screen and challenge a friend.

CONTROLS
• Touch: drag your finger on your half of the screen to move the paddle.
• Keyboard (PC): W/S and the ↑↓ arrow keys.
• Pause, restart and back to menu with a single tap.

FEATURES
• 3 music themes generated in real time (Classic, Energetic and Relaxing) plus retro sound effects.
• Customize paddle colors, light/dark theme and player names.
• Saved statistics: wins, losses, streaks and play time.
• Works offline (installs as an app).
• Full screen and both landscape and portrait modes.

Simple, fast, ad-free fun. Let's play!
```

## Screenshots

Landscape 16:9 format (1280×720 or 1920×1080). Minimum 2, recommended 6.

**How to take them**:
- On the phone: hold power + volume down (Android) or power + home button (iPhone).
- On the PC: open the game in the browser, press F12 → mobile device icon → screenshot button.

**Recommended order**:

1. Main menu: PONG logo + PLAY button.
2. 1 vs AI match: paddles and ball in action.
3. Match setup: "1 player (vs AI)" and "2 players".
4. 2-player match.
5. Sound screen: sliders and theme selector.
6. Victory screen: final score with confetti.
