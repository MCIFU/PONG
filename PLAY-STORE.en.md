The classic arcade Pong, now on your phone and your PC.

CHOOSE YOUR GAME MODE
• 1 player vs the AI, with 3 difficulty levels (Easy, Normal and Hard) that change the speed and intelligence of the machine.
• 2 players: share the screen and challenge a friend.

CONTROLS
• Touch: drag your finger on your half of the screen to move the paddle.
• Keyboard (PC): W/S and the ↑↓ arrow keys.
• Pause, restart and back to menu with a single tap.

FEATURES
• 3 music themes generated in real time (Classic, Energetic and Relaxing) plus retro sound effects.
• Customize paddle colors, light/dark theme and player names.
• Saved statistics: wins, losses, streaks and play time.
• Works offline (installs as an app).
• Full screen and both landscape and portrait modes.

Simple, fast, ad-free fun. Let's play!

## Suggested screenshots

**Format**: landscape 16:9 (1280×720 or 1920×1080), since the game is played in landscape. Minimum 2; I recommend 6.

**How to take them**:
- On the phone: press **power + volume down** at the same time (Android) or **power + home button** (iPhone).
- On the PC: open the game in the browser, press F12 → mobile device icon (Device Toolbar) → screenshot button.

**The 6 recommended screenshots** (in this order):

1. **Main menu**: PONG logo + PLAY button + the three secondary buttons.
2. **1 vs AI match**: paddles and ball in action (against "AI").
3. **Match setup**: screen with "1 player (vs AI)" and "2 players".
4. **2-player match**: player 1 vs player 2.
5. **Sound screen**: music and effects sliders with the theme selector.
6. **Victory screen**: with the final score and confetti.
