PONG

Privacy Policy · Effective date: August 15, 2026

1. Introduction

Pong is an arcade game played against the machine or with two players. This policy explains, in plain terms, what data is collected and how it is used. The short answer is: no personal data is collected.

2. Data we collect

  • No personal data. We do not ask for your name, email, phone number or location.
  • No accounts. No registration is required to play.
  • No data is sent. Nothing is sent to our servers or to third parties.

3. Data stored on your device

The game only stores data on your own device (in the browser's local storage): your preferences and statistics, such as language, volume, colors, music theme and the number of matches won. This data never leaves your device and is never shared with anyone.

4. Third-party services

  • No ads.
  • No analytics or tracking tools.
  • No social media integration.

5. Permissions

The game does not require any special permissions. Controller vibration (if you use one) is optional and only activates when you enable it from the menu.

6. Internet connection

The game works offline. An internet connection is only needed the first time to download it.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, this same page will be updated with the new date.
